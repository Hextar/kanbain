# Work breakdown

Break outcomes into a short tree: 2–4 milestones, 2–4 epics, 2–4 stories per epic, 2–5 leaf tasks per story.

Milestones are calendar commitments, not theme labels. Every card names one milestone.

Epics are outcomes a stakeholder would recognize. Stories are independently demoable slices. Leaf tasks are the work someone can finish in hours, not weeks.

Prefer fewer load-bearing cards over a huge backlog. If two leaves would share the same acceptance criteria, they are one task.

Dependencies may only point at titles that exist in the same plan. Keep the critical path obvious: setup → build → verify → ship.
