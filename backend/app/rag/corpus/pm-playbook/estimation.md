# Estimation

Put a t-shirt, points, and hours on every story and leaf task.

Use hours as the load unit. Points describe relative size. T-shirts communicate uncertainty: XS/S when the work is known, L/XL when discovery remains.

Do not estimate meetings as delivery work. Include tests, review, and rollout in production-grade plans; keep MVP estimates lean.

If the team listed capacity, treat it as hours per week. Sum leaf hours and compare to capacity times weeks until the deadline. If the load does not fit, cut scope, slip the date, or rebalance owners — do not silently overcommit.
